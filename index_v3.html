Approach

From a hard, messy problem to software you can trust: we listen, we model with care, we show the limits, and we ship software people can use with confidence.

Listen first We spend time on the real constraints and the decision you need before any modeling starts.
Model as accurately as required We size the model to the work, not to a showcase method, so it stays useful and honest.
Show the limits We walk through assumptions, sensitivity, and gaps so no one has to guess what the answer can bear.
Solve and hand back an action We turn the model into algorithms, reports, and applications your staff can use again.

About

Some problems are too difficult to solve on your own — too tangled for a spreadsheet and too specific for off-the-shelf software. That is where we work.

We listen until the problem is clear, translate it into a model as accurate as it needs to be, and explain what the model can and cannot do before anyone relies on it. Then we build the analysis and software that put it to work, and we hand back results that tell you what to act on next.
