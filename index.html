Work and Research

Letting schedule optimization

Assigned a year of transportation construction projects to monthly bid lettings while holding district workload, project mix, and seasonal limits inside planning targets.

  • Genetic search over monthly assignments
  • Uncertainty analysis for bids and slippage
  • Reports and a planning application

Sensitivity Analysis

Analysis of how stable a model’s result is when the data behind it shift.

  • Trace a result back to the inputs that drive it
  • Test small and large changes in those inputs
  • Show where the result holds and where it moves

Optimization on GPUs

Work on moving specific optimization problems onto GPUs, using AI techniques where they change what can be solved.

  • Problems selected for the structure the hardware can exploit
  • AI methods paired with the numerical search
