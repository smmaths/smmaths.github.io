Approach

  • Listen firstWe confirm the problem before we model or try to solve it.
  • Model only as accurately as requiredSize the model to the decision — not to a showcase method.
  • Show the limitsWhat the model handles well, what it does not, before anyone relies on it.
  • Solve and hand back an actionAlgorithms, analysis, and — when the model needs a home — practitioner applications.

About

Some problems are too difficult to solve on your own — too tangled for a spreadsheet and too specific for off-the-shelf software. That is where we work.

We listen until the problem is clear, translate it into a model as accurate as it needs to be, and explain what the model can and cannot do before anyone relies on it. Then we build the analysis and software that put it to work, and we hand back results that tell you what to act on next.
